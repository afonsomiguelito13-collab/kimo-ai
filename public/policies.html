Privacy & terms

This app runs on your own device and talks to one company: xKiro. There is no account here, no server of ours, and nothing is collected about you.

Who is involved

Three parties, and it helps to keep them apart:

  • This app — the files on your device. It stores things locally and forwards your messages. It has no backend of its own.
  • xKiro — the API gateway that runs the models. Your messages go here. Their policies govern what happens next.
  • The model vendors — OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek and others, reached through xKiro rather than directly by this app.

What is stored, and where

Everything below lives in your browser's local storage, on your device. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

StoredWhat it is
ChatsYour threads and their messages
API keyOnly if you paste one into Settings
PreferencesModel, theme, system prompt, temperature, token and history limits
BudgetYour credit figure and the running spend estimate

Clearing your browser data, or using Delete all chats in Settings, erases it permanently. There is no backup and no sync — chats do not follow you to another device.

Your API key

The key is kept out of the browser wherever possible:

  • A key in .env stays on the server side and is never sent to the page.
  • A key pasted into Settings is stored in that browser only, and travels to your local server with each request.
  • Either way, the browser never calls xKiro directly — the key is attached server-side.
  • The Test key result only ever shows a masked fragment, never the whole key.

A key in Settings overrides the one in .env. Anyone with access to your unlocked device can reach that browser's stored key. Prefer .env if that matters to you, and revoke a key you suspect is exposed at xkiro.com/dashboard/api/keys.

What gets sent when you chat

Each message sends to xKiro: the recent messages in that thread (as many as your history window allows), your system prompt, any images you attach, and the model and sampling settings. Older messages beyond the window are not sent.

Temporary chats are never written to local storage — closing one loses it for good. They are still sent to xKiro to get a reply, so a temporary chat is private on your device, not invisible to the provider.

Treat anything you send as leaving your device

Once a message reaches xKiro, what happens to it is governed by their retention and training policies and those of the underlying vendor — not by this app. Do not paste passwords, financial details, medical records, or anyone else's personal information. Read xkiro.com for the terms you are actually agreeing to.

Web search

When you switch on the magnifier in the composer, your search query is sent to DuckDuckGo, and to Wikipedia if DuckDuckGo returns nothing useful. The query travels through your local server, so those services see your server's request rather than your browser directly, but the text of the query does leave your device.

  • Only the message you send is used as the query — not your chat history.
  • Nothing is sent to either service while the toggle is off.
  • Retrieved snippets are attached to your conversation and therefore also go to xKiro.
  • No key or account is used for search, and neither service is told who you are by this app.

Money

Your billing relationship is with xKiro. The credit budget in Settings is a local convenience: it estimates spend from reported token usage so you can watch it move. It cannot see your balance, does not update your account, and may drift from what you are really charged.

Your xKiro dashboard is the only authoritative record of spending. Do not rely on the figure in this app for anything that matters.
